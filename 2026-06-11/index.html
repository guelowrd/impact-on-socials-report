Lessons for Future Campaigns

What worked:
  • Discrete product/news anchors drive most of the reach. 7 of the top 10 X tweets in 44 days hit 15K+ — they were: the launch article, the mainnet tease, the Operators announcement, episode launches with named guests, the Libra/Diem thesis. Abstract narrative tweets averaged 3–5K.
  • The Privacy Podcast became a reliable reach engine. Three episodes hit 13K, 24K, 41K impressions. Anchored content with named guests outperformed everything else over the full window.
  • Substance-first LinkedIn explainers are the highest-leverage format. May 12 paradox post (46% ER, 99 clicks) and May 5 Salon recap (32% ER, 93 clicks) are the all-time top LI posts. Every campaign should produce 2–3 of these.
What didn't:
  • The campaign didn't grow the audience. X followers net −41 during the campaign (slowed from −203 baseline). LI followers per day flat (2.5 vs 2.67). The reach went to people who didn't follow afterwards.
  • LinkedIn page views dropped 33% (35.2 → 23.7/day) despite feed reach being flat-to-up. The campaign content didn't drive people to the page itself.
  • Pure-narrative tweets underperformed by a wide margin. Inspirational quotes and abstract thesis tweets averaged 2–4K impressions; tweets pointing to concrete substance (livestream, article, partner, episode) averaged 10K+.
  • 44 days may be too long. Reach was lumpy — concentrated in 4–5 moments (launch article, livestream, two podcast eps, Operators). The space between moments diluted attention without compounding it.
Principles for the Wallet campaign:
  • Anchor on news beats, not narrative. Plan 4–5 discrete "we shipped X" / "Y joined" / "here's how it works" moments in advance — those are the only things that consistently hit 15K+ on X. Narrative wraps around them; it can't carry them alone.
  • Solve the follower conversion problem. A campaign that doubles reach but loses followers means the bio, pinned content, and follow-up content aren't doing their job. Update the X and LinkedIn pages before Wallet launches so new visitors have a reason to follow.
  • Front-load LinkedIn explainers. The paradox + Salon posts prove the format works. Plan 3+ "how it works / why it matters" LI posts for the Wallet window.
  • Tighten the window. Consider a 2–3 week core campaign window with the major beats clustered, then trail off — instead of the 6-week Guardian arc where attention dispersed.
